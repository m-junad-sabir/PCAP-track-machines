# Surveys, TPV and Design Optimization

> The source defines *that* these activities exist and who does them, but gives **no questionnaires, fields or workflows**. Do not invent them; see OQ-04.

## Surveys (FE, Android app)

| Survey | Population | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Farmers, before delivery of the 5,000 machines | Before delivery |
| Midline | 500 farmers (10% of baseline farmers) | After 1 year of machine use |
| Endline | 500 farmers | After 2 years of machine use |

- Objective: develop survey tools (questionnaires) and data collection.
- FEs see survey status for the district allotted to them (§6.1.1).

## TPV of rental subsidy (FE, Android app)

- Scope: **1,000 farmers** who obtained machines on **rent**.
- Verifies rental subsidy of **Rs. 5,000 per acre, ceiling 25 acres**.
- Objective: develop a questionnaire to collect rental-subsidy data and conduct TPV.
- FEs see rental-subsidy status for their district.
- Payment/disbursement processing is out of scope.

## Design optimization

- Goal: incorporate suggested modifications to the super seeder's technical specification to **reduce weight** (currently >1,000 kg) and **cost**.
- A **Design Engineer** enters progress of the modifications (surface and data fields unspecified).

## Known gaps
Questionnaire content, sampling/selection method for the 500 and 1,000 farmers, link to Farmer master / machine records, GPS/photo requirements, and design-progress fields are all unspecified.
